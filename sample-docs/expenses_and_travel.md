# Expenses & Travel Policy


**Owner:** Finance · **Effective:** 1 February 2024 · **Version:** 2.7


## 1. Submitting expenses


Expenses must be submitted through **Expensify within 30 days** of purchase.
Receipts are required for any expense **over $25**. Reimbursement is paid with
the next payroll cycle after approval, typically **within 10 business days**.


## 2. Approval thresholds


| Amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Under $500 | Direct manager |
| $500 – $2,500 | Director |
| Over $2,500 | VP + Finance |


## 3. Travel


- Economy class for flights **under 6 hours**; premium economy is allowed for
  flights **over 6 hours**.
- Hotel nightly cap: **200** elsewhere. Higher rates
  require pre-approval.
- Meals: **100 per day** international.
- Ground transport: rideshare or public transit preferred; rental cars need
  manager approval.


## 4. Conferences


Conference attendance requires manager approval and a written summary shared
with the team within **one week** of returning. Registration up to **$2,000**
is covered without additional approval.


## 5. Non-reimbursable items


Personal entertainment, minibar, airline lounge access, upgrades, fines,
and alcohol (except at approved company events) are **not reimbursable**.


## 6. Corporate cards


Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than **four times per
year**. Personal charges on a corporate card must be repaid within **14 days**.
