# Remote Work & Work-From-Home Policy


**Owner:** People Operations · **Effective:** 1 March 2024 · **Version:** 4.1


## 1. Eligibility


All full-time employees who have completed their **90-day probationary period**
are eligible for remote work. Contractors and interns require written approval
from their manager and a member of the People Operations team.


## 2. How many days can I work from home?


Employees may work from home **up to three (3) days per week**. At least
**two (2) days per week must be spent in the office** for team collaboration.
Teams may agree on fixed "anchor days" — the standard company anchor days are
**Tuesday and Thursday**.


Fully remote arrangements (5 days per week) require **VP-level approval** and a
signed Remote Work Agreement. Fully remote employees must attend on-site
company events at least **twice per year**.


## 3. Core hours


Regardless of location, all employees must be reachable during **core hours of
10:00–16:00 in their local time zone**. Outside core hours, schedules are
flexible as long as the weekly hours target of 40 is met.


## 4. Requesting a remote arrangement


1. Submit a request in the HR portal at least **10 business days** in advance.
2. Manager approval is required within 5 business days.
3. Recurring schedules are reviewed **quarterly**.


Occasional ad-hoc remote days do **not** require a formal request — a message in
your team channel is sufficient.


## 5. Equipment and expenses


The company provides a **laptop and a one-time home-office stipend of $500**.
The stipend may be used for a monitor, chair, desk or headset. Internet costs
are **not** reimbursed. Equipment remains company property and must be returned
within 30 days of offboarding.


## 6. Security requirements for remote work


- Company laptops must use **full-disk encryption** and the approved VPN.
- Public Wi-Fi requires the VPN at all times.
- Work must never be performed on shared or family devices.
- See the Information Security Policy for the full list of requirements.


## 7. Tax and location changes


Employees who relocate to a different country or state must notify People
Operations **at least 30 days before the move**, as tax registration may take
time. Relocation to a country where the company has no legal entity is
generally not supported.


## 8. Non-compliance


Repeated failure to meet in-office anchor days or core-hour availability may
result in the remote arrangement being revoked.
